Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 4, 2026

1. Introduction

Bingo Home Buyers ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our services. Please read this policy carefully. By using our website, you consent to the practices described herein.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information

We may collect personally identifiable information that you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Submit a contact form or request a cash offer
  • Call us or send us an email
  • Subscribe to our communications
  • Opt in to receive SMS/text messages from us

This information may include your name, email address, phone number, property address, and details about your property or situation.

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect certain information, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, and the dates and times of your visits. We may also use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to collect this information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect for purposes including:

  • Responding to your inquiries and providing cash offers on your property
  • Processing real estate transactions
  • Sending SMS/text messages related to your inquiry, cash offer, or transaction (if you have opted in)
  • Communicating with you about our services
  • Improving our website and customer experience
  • Complying with legal obligations
  • Detecting and preventing fraud

4. SMS/Text Messaging Policy

Consent: By providing your phone number and opting in, you consent to receive SMS/text messages from Bingo Home Buyers. Consent is not a condition of purchase or service. You may opt in by submitting your phone number through our website contact form, by texting us directly, or by providing verbal or written consent.

Message Types: Messages may include responses to your inquiries, cash offer details, appointment confirmations, transaction updates, and follow-up communications related to our home-buying services.

Message Frequency: Message frequency varies based on your interaction with us. You may receive up to 10 messages per month.

Message and Data Rates: Standard message and data rates may apply. Please contact your wireless carrier for details about your messaging plan.

Opt-Out: You may opt out of receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive from us. After opting out, you will receive a one-time confirmation message and no further texts will be sent unless you re-subscribe.

Help: For help or questions about our text messaging program, reply HELP to any message or contact us at info@bingohomebuyers.com or (614) 964-5684.

No Sharing: We will not sell, rent, or share your phone number or opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for promotional or marketing purposes. Your phone number will only be used by Bingo Home Buyers for the purposes described in this policy.

Supported carriers include but are not limited to: AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon, Sprint, U.S. Cellular, and other major U.S. carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Service providers: Third parties that assist us in operating our website, conducting our business, or servicing you (e.g., title companies, attorneys, contractors)

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Request correction of inaccurate information
