    FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How does selling my house for cash work?
    It's simple. You contact us with your property details, we evaluate your home and present a fair, no-obligation cash offer within 24 hours. If you accept, we handle all the paperwork and close on your timeline. No showings, no open houses, no waiting for buyer financing.
    How fast can you close?
    We can close in as little as 7–14 days, depending on your situation. Since we pay cash, there's no waiting on bank approvals or mortgage underwriting. If you need more time, we're happy to work around your schedule.
    Do I need to make repairs before selling?
    Not at all. We buy houses in any condition, whether it needs a new roof, has foundation issues, or just needs a deep clean. You won't need to spend a dime on repairs, inspections, or staging. We take care of everything after closing.
    Are there any fees or commissions?
    Zero. There are no agent commissions, no closing costs, and no hidden fees. The cash offer we give you is the amount you walk away with. We cover all closing costs ourselves.
    How do you determine your offer price?
    We look at recent comparable sales in your area, the current condition of your property, and local market trends. Our goal is to give you a fair offer that reflects your home's true value, minus the hassle, time, and costs of a traditional sale.
